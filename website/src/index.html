Why Flint

Small checks. A fire that grows.

A habit grows from what you do each day, and it is easy to lose. Flint shows every day you keep, so you see the fire build, and you notice when it starts to dim.

01

Private

No ads or analytics. Your habits live in a database on your iPhone. An account is optional: sign in only to sync your devices.

02

Stacks

Link habits that follow each other. A stack is complete when every habit in it is checked on the same day.

03

Rewards

Habits you choose earn coins, complete stacks earn a bonus, and you spend them on rewards you define.

How it works

Check in. Keep it lit.

  1. Add a habitdone once a day, or counted with amounts and notes
  2. Check it offin the app, from a widget, with Shortcuts, or through your AI assistant
  3. Link a stackhabits that follow each other
  4. Claim a rewardwith the coins you earned
