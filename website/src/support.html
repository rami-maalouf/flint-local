Support

Updated November 8, 2026

Something not working, or an idea for Flint? Open an issue on GitHub, and say which iPhone and iOS version you use. Leave out your email address and other personal details: issues are public.

Your data

Your habits live on your iPhone. To keep a copy or move them, use Export Data in Settings, then Import Data on the other iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its data, unless you sync with an account or kept a backup.

Account and sync

Sign in under Settings, then Account & Sync, to keep your devices in step. If Account & Sync shows Sign in again, your session has ended: sign in once more.

AI assistants

To use Flint from Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant that supports connectors, add Flint with the address https://habits.ramimaalouf.com/mcp, then sign in with your Flint account. To remove access, use the connections page.

Reminders

Reminders and missed-day alerts need permission for notifications. If they do not arrive, check Flint in the Settings app, under Notifications.

Privacy

What Flint does with your data is in the privacy policy.
