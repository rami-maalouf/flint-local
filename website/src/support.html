Support

Updated November 7, 2026

Something not working, or an idea for Flint Local? Open an issue on GitHub, and say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Your data

Your habits live on your iPhone. To keep a copy or move them, use Export Data in Settings, then import the file on the other iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its data, unless iCloud sync is on or you kept a backup.

Reminders

Reminders and missed-day alerts need permission for notifications. If they do not arrive, check Flint Local in the Settings app, under Notifications.
