Privacy

Updated November 8, 2026

Flint is built so that your habits stay yours. It has no ads and no analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites. You can use it without an account.

What stays on your iPhone

Your habits, check-ins, notes, stacks, coins and rewards are stored in a database on your iPhone. The app's widgets and Shortcuts read and write that same database. Without an account, the developer cannot see any of it.

Account and sync

An account is optional. If you create one in Settings, under Account & Sync, Flint keeps a copy of your habit data on its sync server, so your devices stay in step. That server, run by the developer, stores your email address, what it needs to check your password, and the habit data you sync. It does not sell or share that data. The sign-in session on your iPhone is kept in the iPhone's secure storage.

AI assistants

You can connect an AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT, to your Flint account. You sign in and approve each connection yourself. A connected assistant can read your habits, check-ins, stacks, coins and rewards. If you also allow changes, it can record or undo check-ins and claim rewards. What you share with an assistant is also covered by its maker's privacy policy. You can disconnect an assistant at any time on the connections page, and the change takes effect at once. When the last assistant is disconnected, the copy kept for assistants is deleted.

Backups

Export Data in Settings makes a backup file. It goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Notifications

Reminders and missed-day alerts are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

App updates

Flint checks Expo's update service for fixes to the app itself. That request carries technical details, such as the app version and platform, and none of your habit data.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this site, your email address and the platforms you picked are stored in a private Notion database. They are used only to tell you when Flint is ready for them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the data on your iPhone. To delete your account, its synced data or your waitlist entry, open an issue on GitHub without your email address in it, and the developer will reply there to confirm it is you.

Children

Flint is not made for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect data from them.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
