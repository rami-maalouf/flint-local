Privacy

Updated November 7, 2026

Flint Local is built so that your habits stay yours. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not track you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your habits, check-ins, notes, stacks, coins and rewards are stored in a database on your iPhone. The app's widgets and Shortcuts read and write that same database. The developer cannot see it.

iCloud sync

Sync is off until you turn it on. When it is on, Flint Local copies your data to your own private iCloud database, through Apple's CloudKit, so your other devices can read it. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud. The developer cannot see that data.

Backups

Export Data in Settings makes a backup file. It goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Notifications

Reminders and missed-day alerts are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

App updates

Flint Local checks Expo's update service for fixes to the app itself. That request carries technical details, such as the app version and platform, and none of your habit data.
