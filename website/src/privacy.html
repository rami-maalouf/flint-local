Privacy

Updated November 20, 2026

Flint is built so that your habits stay yours. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites. The developer runs no server for the app and cannot see your habits.

What stays on your iPhone

Your habits, check-ins, notes, stacks, coins and rewards are stored in a database on your iPhone. The app's widgets and Shortcuts read and write that same database.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off until you turn it on in Settings, under iCloud Sync. When it is on, Flint keeps a copy of your habit data in your own private iCloud database, so your devices stay in step. That database belongs to your iCloud account: Apple stores it, and the developer cannot access it. Turning sync off keeps all your data on your iPhone.

Optional donation

Flint is free. Settings has an optional one-time donation. Apple processes the payment through your Apple Account, and the developer does not receive your payment details. Flint checks the purchase on your iPhone only to say thank you and to restore that thank-you. A donation unlocks nothing.

Backups

Export Data in Settings makes a backup file. It goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Notifications

Reminders and missed-day alerts are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

App updates

When it starts, Flint checks Expo's update service (EAS Update) for fixes to the app itself. That request carries the platform, the app's runtime version and update channel, the IDs of the app updates it has, and a random install ID that the app makes on your iPhone. It carries none of your habit data.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this site, your email address and the platforms you picked are stored in a private Notion database. They are used only to tell you when Flint is ready for them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the data on your iPhone. A copy you synced stays in your private iCloud database until you delete it in your iCloud storage settings. To delete your waitlist entry, open an issue on GitHub without your email address in it, and the developer will reply there to confirm it is you.

Children

Flint is not made for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect data from them.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
